SWARM_DEMO_DECISIONS_SWARM_COORDINATION
=======================================

Date: 2026-05-25
Category: Multi-drone response policy and coverage continuity

1) Detector/support policy (Case 2)
- If Drone 1 detects fire:
  - Drone 2 is preferred as responder/overwatch.
  - Drone 3 is preferred as backfill.
- Reason: consistent, easy-to-explain demo choreography.

2) Backfill policy
- Backfill drone covers both:
  - its own sector
  - the temporarily vacated support drone sector
- Implemented as combined waypoint route (dual-sector loop).
- Reason: preserve area coverage while response drone leaves patrol.

3) Response posture
- Tracker + overwatch orbit around incident for persistent monitoring.
- Backfill remains on patrol route instead of converging to incident.
- Reason: show cooperative monitoring + distributed coverage in one scene.
