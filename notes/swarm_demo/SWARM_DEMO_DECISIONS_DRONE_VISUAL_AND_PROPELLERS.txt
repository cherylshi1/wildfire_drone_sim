SWARM_DEMO_DECISIONS_DRONE_VISUAL_AND_PROPELLERS
================================================

Date: 2026-05-25
Category: Drone visuals, propeller placement, render tuning

1) Propeller placement coordinate-space decision
- Rotor anchor bounds are computed in drone-model local space:
  getTightBounds(drone_model)
- Reason: avoid double-scaling/offset drift after parent rotation/scale.

2) Vertical placement correction
- Lowered rotor plane with local Y nudge:
  DRONE_PROPELLER_Y_NUDGE_LOCAL = -0.45
- Reason: user-reported "bottom too high" after front alignment looked correct.

3) Tuning knobs for manual placement edits
- DRONE_PROPELLER_X_OFFSET_FACTOR
  - arm spread left/right
- DRONE_PROPELLER_Z_OFFSET_FACTOR
  - arm spread front/back
- DRONE_PROPELLER_Y_TOP_MARGIN_FACTOR
  - baseline height from top envelope
- DRONE_PROPELLER_X_NUDGE_LOCAL
  - shift all rotors together in local X
- DRONE_PROPELLER_Y_NUDGE_LOCAL
  - shift all rotors together in local Y (negative lowers)
- DRONE_PROPELLER_Z_NUDGE_LOCAL
  - shift all rotors together in local Z

4) Practical tweak order
- Step 1: keep X/Z offsets if footprint looks right.
- Step 2: adjust Y_NUDGE_LOCAL by small increments (0.05-0.15).
- Step 3: only then adjust Y_TOP_MARGIN_FACTOR for larger height moves.
